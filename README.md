# Dickinson
![Genre](https://img.shields.io/badge/Genre-Comedy%20Drama-purple)
**Dickinson** is a historical comedy-drama series about the young poet *Emily Dickinson*, starring **Hailee Steinfeld**.
## Quick Facts
- **Starring:** Hailee Steinfeld as Emily Dickinson
- **Genre:** Comedy-drama, period piece
- **Seasons:** 3
- **Platform:** Apple TV+
## Why I Like It
1. Modern music and humor in a 19th-century setting
2. Creative and colorful visuals
3. Strong lead performance by Hailee Steinfeld
> "I'm Nobody! Who are you?" is one of Emily Dickinson's famous poems.
